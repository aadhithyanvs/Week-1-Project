# Employee Attrition Analysis – Findings Summary

## 1. Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to analyse employee attrition using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset. The analysis aims to identify patterns and workplace factors associated with employees leaving the company.

## 2. Dataset Description

The project uses the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset, containing 1,470 employee records and 35 columns. It includes employee demographics, department, job role, monthly income, work experience and attrition status.

Data inspection found no missing values or duplicate records. Data cleaning and exploratory data analysis were performed using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib and Seaborn.

## 3. Three Key Insights

1. **Department-wise attrition:** Sales has the highest attrition rate at approximately 20.63%, followed by Human Resources at 19.05% and Research & Development at 13.84%.

2. **Monthly income and attrition:** Employees who left had an average monthly income of approximately 4,787, compared with approximately 6,833 for employees who stayed.

3. **Income and job level:** Monthly Income and Job Level have a strong positive correlation of approximately 0.95, indicating that employees at higher job levels generally have higher monthly incomes.

## 4. Two Recommendations

1. Investigate the reasons for higher employee attrition in the Sales department and develop suitable employee retention strategies.

2. Review compensation, career growth opportunities and employee satisfaction to identify possible factors associated with employee attrition.

## 5. Limitations

- The analysis is based on 1,470 employee records and may not represent the entire workforce.
- Correlation does not establish cause and effect.
- Some factors influencing employee attrition may not be available in the dataset.
- The department budget values used in the merge are illustrative and are not actual company financial figures.

## Conclusion

The analysis identified differences in attrition across departments and income groups. These findings can help the company investigate employee retention challenges and develop better workforce strategies.
